# Branding

Voice guide for Ibraheem Farooq’s site. Future copy should follow this. The layout stays. The words change.

## Position

Product engineer with taste.

He owns the problem, the design, and the code that ships. Design is judgment. Engineering is how the product gets built. Taste is the standard: a product that works and feels careless is unfinished.

He likes design engineering. The person who decides how something feels should be able to make it.

## Audience

People hiring a product engineer, and people who want to work with him on a product. Lead with the hireable product engineer. The site does not sell freelance services.

## Voice

- First person.
- Short sentences.
- Plain words.
- Say what he believes, how he works, and what he did.
- Taste leads. Then pace. Then who he is.

How he works: small teams, close to the user, ship a thin version, iterate. He has taken products from zero to one. He has managed when the work needed a team.

## Words to use

product, design, taste, ship, build, iterate, small team, zero to one

Format tags that already exist may stay: Landing Page Design, Mobile App Design.

## Words to remove

- UI/UX, UX, user-centered, intuitive interfaces
- Branding, brand identity, visual branding, when they describe him or his practice
- Freelance service language: a Services menu, “I offer”, passionate, seamless, robust, innovative
- Present, when marking a job. He is not employed.

“Brand” may appear only when a sentence would be false without it. Prefer product and design.

## Homepage

Hero, exact line: `A product engineer with taste.`

The name animates first. About 0.5s after that animation ends, the line rises one word at a time, from 18px, in 0.29s, with 0.16s between words, and an ease-out. On desktop it sits on the same row as the nav, at the top of the hero.

On load the black hero fills the screen: no inset, no corner radius. While the name rises, the inset and the radius settle together to 8px and 10px, with an ease-out. Scrolling moves the hero with the page. The name and the line inside it move slower, and the frame clips them.

## Projects

Design projects stay design projects. Do not retell a design project as an engineering project.

Concepts stay concepts. Split and Pulse Active are concepts. Say so.

Upping the Ante is a design project. Its only tag is Design. Do not call it branding.

Other projects keep their format tag (Landing Page Design or Mobile App Design) and use Product and Design in place of UI/UX and branding.

Summaries name the product problem and the design decision. One or two sentences. No invented metrics.

## Before shipping copy

- Could this sentence sit on a generic designer’s site? Rewrite it.
- Does it claim he shipped code he did not ship? Remove the claim.
- Does it use UI/UX or branding? Remove the word.
- Does About name a company or a product? Take it out.
